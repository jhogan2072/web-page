8-Week Knee Rehab Program

Prepared by Madison O'Malley · 175 Cambridge St, Suite 470, Boston, MA · 617-643-9999

✓  Week 1 complete — 5 exercises
3 sets of 10, 3–4 times per week unless otherwise noted. Rest 30–90 seconds between sets, depending on level of exertion.
01

Single Leg Bridge

Setup

Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat on the floor. Lift one leg off the floor, bending that knee to a 90-degree angle.

Movement

Engage your stomach muscles and slowly lift your hips into a bridge position. Hold briefly, then lower back down and repeat.

Form Check

Keep core engaged throughout. Don't allow your back to arch or your hips to drop to either side.

TipKeep your stomach muscles engaged the entire time — this is what protects your lower back and keeps your hips level.
Watch video demo
02

Squat

Increase difficulty by holding a dumbbell between your hands

Setup

Stand upright with your feet slightly wider than shoulder-width apart.

Movement

Bend at your knees and hips, squatting down until your knees approach a 90-degree angle. Straighten your legs and repeat.

Form Check

Keep your back straight. Do not let your knees travel forward past your toes.

TipThink of sitting back into a chair — keep weight in your heels and your chest tall throughout the movement.
Watch video demo
03

Side Stepping with Resistance at Ankles

Setup

Stand upright with a resistance band looped around your ankles. Bend your knees slightly into a mini squat position.

Movement

Slowly step sideways, maintaining tension in the band throughout. Step one direction for your reps, then return.

Form Check

Keep feet pointing straight forward. Do not let your knees collapse inward at any point during the movement.

TipKeep the band taut the whole time — if it goes slack you're stepping too close together and losing the hip strengthening benefit.
Watch video demo
04

Lateral Step Down

Setup

Stand on a platform or step with one leg, the other leg hanging off the side edge.

Movement

Maintaining an upright posture, bend at your knee and hip to slowly lower the hanging foot toward the ground. Return to start and repeat.

Form Check

Do not let your trunk lean forward or your standing knee collapse inward as you lower down.

TipControl the descent — slow is better. Most of the benefit comes from the eccentric (lowering) phase.
Watch video demo
05

Runner's Step Up/Down

Perform on 12″ step, or 8″ step with added weight

Setup

Stand with one foot resting on a platform or step in front of you, the other foot on the floor.

Movement

Slowly step up, raising your opposite knee to a 90-degree angle while swinging your opposite arm forward simultaneously. Lower back down and repeat.

Form Check

Do not let your trunk lean forward or your driving knee collapse inward as you lower back to the floor.

TipThe arm swing is intentional — it mimics natural running mechanics and helps load the hip and core correctly.

06

Single Leg Knee Extension

New
Weight machine · Should not be painful — reduce weight or skip if it is

Setup

Sit on the machine with feet under the roller pad. Set your weight, check seat position (knees at seat edge), and position the roller pad just above your ankles.

Movement

Engage your quadriceps to lift the roller pad with one foot, straightening your leg. Slowly lower back down and repeat. Then switch legs.

Form Check

Keep movements slow and controlled. Do not allow your knee to lock out fully at the top of the movement.

ImportantThis exercise should not be painful. If you feel pain during the movement, reduce the weight. If pain persists, skip this exercise and let your PT know.
TipEngage the platform lock every time you finish — don't skip this step.
Watch video demo
07

Romanian Deadlift (RDL)

New

Setup

Stand with feet hip-width apart, holding a barbell or dumbbells in front of your thighs. Retract your shoulder blades, brace your core, and maintain the natural curve of your lower back.

Movement

Initiate by driving your hips backward — not by reaching down with your arms. Keep a slight bend in your knees throughout. Lower the weight along your legs until you feel a strong hamstring stretch (roughly mid-shin), then drive your hips forward to return to standing.

Form Check

Keep the bar or weights close to your legs the entire way down. Shins stay nearly vertical. Stop if you feel your lower back rounding — you've gone too far.

TipThink "hips back," not "chest down." The movement comes from hinging at the hips, not bending your spine. Stop at the hamstring stretch, not when the weight reaches the floor.
Watch video demo
08

Single Leg Press

New
Weight machine

Setup

Load the machine with your desired weight. Sit with your back flat against the backrest and both feet planted on the platform hip-width apart or slightly wider. Unlock the machine by pushing up on the platform and pulling the lever toward you.

Movement

Remove one foot from the platform. Bring your knee toward your chest to slowly lower the weight as far as you can while keeping your hips against the seat. Press through your foot to straighten your leg without locking your knee, and repeat. Then switch legs.

Form Check

Keep movements slow and controlled. Do not let your knee collapse inward or travel forward past your toes. Engage the platform lock when finished.

TipDon't rush the lowering phase — controlling the weight on the way down is where much of the strengthening happens. Always re-engage the platform lock before removing your foot from the platform.

09

Split Squats Upright Trunk (Quad Bias)

New

Setup

Begin standing. Step forward with one leg, keeping your feet hip-width apart. Keep your trunk upright with your ears, shoulders, and hips aligned.

Movement

Lower your body straight down, using the front leg to do most of the work. Return to the upright split stance position and repeat.

Form Check

Keep your trunk upright throughout — do not lean forward. You should feel this exercise on the outside of your thigh.

TipKeep your trunk upright and you should feel this exercise on the outside of your thigh.
Watch video demo
10

Single Leg RDL

New
Can use a foam roller for balance assist

Setup

Begin in a comfortable standing position.

Movement

Maintaining a straight spine, reach down toward the ground while reaching your leg back. Think about pushing your heel toward the back wall and keeping your hips level throughout. Slowly return to standing position.

Form Check

Keep your abdominal muscles engaged throughout. Keep your hips level — don't let the lifted side rotate open.

TipBe mindful to keep your abdominal muscles engaged throughout the exercise.
Watch video demo
11

Hip Abduction With Resistance Loop

New

Setup

Stand upright with a resistance band looped around your ankles, holding a stable object for balance.

Movement

Lift one leg diagonally, keeping your knee straight. Return to the starting position and repeat. Complete all reps on one side, then switch.

Form Check

Keep your abdominals tight and your back straight during the exercise. Do not let your trunk lean to the side.

TipKeep your abdominals tight and your back straight during the exercise.
Watch video demo
12

Single Leg Squat with Chair Touch

New

Setup

Begin in a standing upright position in front of a chair.

Movement

Lift one leg off the ground and lower yourself into a squatting position, bending at your hips and knees until you lightly touch the chair. Return to a standing position and repeat.

Form Check

Maintain your balance throughout. Do not let your knee bend forward past your toes.

TipMaintain your balance during the exercise and do not let your knee bend forward past your toes.
